# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## Primary Target

**SMBs drowning in manual processes who know they need AI but lack internal expertise.**

---

## Company Fit

| Attribute | Target | Why |
|-----------|--------|-----|
| **Size** | 20-200 employees | Big enough to have pain, small enough to decide fast |
| **Revenue** | $2M-$50M | Can afford services, not enterprise procurement hell |
| **Tech maturity** | Cloud-based tools (Slack, Google Workspace, etc.) | Ready to integrate AI |

## Industries (Ranked)

1. **Professional Services** — Law firms, accounting, consulting (document-heavy)
2. **Healthcare** — Clinics, practices (admin burden crushing margins)
3. **Real Estate** — Brokerages, property mgmt (repetitive workflows)
4. **Financial Services** — Wealth mgmt, insurance (compliance + reporting)
5. **E-commerce** — DTC brands (customer service, fulfillment)
6. **Manufacturing** — Mid-size (supply chain, inventory)

## Decision Makers

| Title | Role in Sale |
|-------|--------------|
| **CEO / Founder** | Final decision, budget holder (primary) |
| **COO / VP Ops** | Owns the pain, champion (primary) |
| **CTO / IT Director** | Technical validation (secondary) |

## Buying Signals

- Hiring ops/admin roles (process pain)
- Recent funding round (has budget)
- Job posts mentioning "automation" or "efficiency"
- Using Zapier/Make (outgrown DIY automation)
- Complaints about manual work on LinkedIn/reviews

## Disqualifiers

- Under 10 employees (can't afford)
- Over 500 employees (sales cycle too long)
- No cloud tools (not ready)
- Already has internal AI/ML team
- Government/heavily regulated (slow procurement)

## Geographic Focus

**Phase 1:** US (EST timezone priority for Ryan's availability)
**Phase 2:** Canada, UK, Australia (English-speaking)

---

## Messaging Angle

> "You know AI can help. You don't have time to figure it out. We do it for you."

**Pain points to hit:**
- Drowning in repetitive tasks
- Competitors moving faster
- Can't hire fast enough
- Tried DIY automation, hit limits
